### ทิศทางภาพร่วมกัน

Use case: stylized-concept. Project: Ayothaya Survival, original fantasy RPG inspired by old Siam, rendered as sophisticated hand-pixeled 32-bit anime/chibi RPG artwork. Gameplay character concept anchors, not production animation. Common camera: orthographic three-quarter top-down game camera with a 35-degree downward pitch. All subjects face south, slightly toward the camera; both front and a small amount of top surfaces are visible. Tiny dark aubergine outlines only on key silhouettes, clusters of warm sunlit pixels from upper left, smooth internal color transitions made with pixel clusters, relatively muted deep jungle and terracotta palettes offset by clear golden highlights; cloth has a few authentic-looking folds. Ground-facing stance, lively faces, sturdy silhouette and clear hands/feet. Human bodies are 3 heads tall, with full figures and clear shapes at low game resolution. The visual vocabulary is a fantasy river town of old Siam: wrapped cloth, sabai, chong kraben, woven materials, patinated bronze and laterite, with imaginary details that belong to the same world. Match art across the set. Keep pixels hard-edged and legible as a nearest-neighbor upscale of a sprite; avoid blur, photorealism, smooth vector outlines, plastic 3D surfaces. True transparent alpha behind the artwork. No text, no diagrams, no borders, no frames, no backdrops, no logos, no ground disks, no cast shadows, no cutoffs.

### ตัวละครผู้เล่น

Wide rectangular composition with two large separate 3/4-front subjects, equally prominent, with feet aligned to a common baseline and a generous empty gap between them. They are distinct appearances for the same beginner river-town swordfighter class. LEFT: Khram, a lean Thai young adult man with warm tan skin, steady friendly eyebrows, a high little swept-back topknot with a small indigo tie, a fitted indigo cross-wrapped short shirt with one sleeve folded to elbow and a modest golden edging, a wide cinnabar red woven waist sash with a hanging asymmetric end, plain earth-brown chong kraben with indigo wraps on one lower leg, woven sandals. Holds a short blade shaped like a local daab lowered at his right side, gleaming steel inlaid with a simple bronze collar; grounded alert posture. RIGHT: Mali, a young Thai woman with warm brown skin, calm confident brows and a black bun striped by a red thread, a faded old-brick burgundy sabai draped diagonally across a warm cream blouse, deep jade loose chong kraben, ochre-gold waist tie and softer weighted hip stance, a simple lowered steel daab at opposite side, no jewelry except a tiny brass earring. Equipment reads as first-rank cloth armor made by people of a riverside market. No heroic magic, no capes. Full body at the same visible physical height, pixel edges sculpted with small natural variation. The characters must look like richly expressive 2D game characters. Leave at least 10% clear transparent margin around both silhouettes.

### NPC

Landscape composition exactly three clearly separated full-body townspeople in one horizontal row, no adjacent contact, three evenly spaced centers, feet aligned. Silhouettes must be immediately recognizable without comparing costume colors. LEFT: Lung Kham, a wiry elderly Thai riverside town headman, pale gray loose topknot, silver eyebrows, kind deep-set eyes, stooped posture accentuated by a crooked tamarind wood walking staff taller than him with a tied small straw charm, ivory shoulder cloth over weathered pale burnt-saffron and ochre wrap trousers, bare ankles and woven sandals. MIDDLE: an assertive friendly middle-aged woman herb merchant in jade and dusty lilac with broad palm-leaf woven hat as main silhouette, small reed pack and holds a handwoven basket containing green healing leaves and a tiny orange-red glazed clay pot, delightfully expressive eyes, plain role-appropriate cloth. RIGHT: a broad-shouldered wharf gate watchman in muted oxblood red with antique bronze chest clasps and dark taupe shoulder bands, short decorated dark headcloth, upright spear and compact oval wicker shield, wrapped calves and broad neutral stance. Everyday fantasy town, all faces and all feet visible. Spear and staff do not run into another cell. Character size and detailing match 3-heads-tall anime RPG sprites rather than realistic figurine proportions.

### มอนสเตอร์

Four Thai fantasy creatures in an orderly 2-by-2 transparent layout, one per quadrant, ample space between silhouettes with equal safe margins. Different body proportions and visible sizes are intentional. TOP LEFT: juvenile tamarind spirit, the smallest at approximately one half human height, clearly resembling an actual curved segmented tamarind pod with a raised crescent hooked end, matte tawny chestnut shell, little clawless stubby feet and clear warm amber eyes in one dark break in the woody shell, two delicate tamarind leaf springs pointing up. Low, whimsical, mischievous and easy to distinguish from a generic slime or pear. TOP RIGHT: a copper brown dusky Thai macaque forest creature, low grounded crouch, cream cheek fur and little rust-red knotted wrist wrap, expressive dark mask, a raised almost circular hooked tail with a strong readable contour and ears at peak; body clearly longer than it is tall. BOTTOM LEFT: pale river-forest spirit, vertical silhouette that tapers into floating layered mint fabric and two reed leaves, dark translucent teal shadow clusters, two pale jade eyes in a simple rounded smooth face, small folded water lily at the chest, looming airy sleeves, a distinct strong top-to-bottom elegant shape with vertical space under its body, no full opaque human legs. BOTTOM RIGHT: hulking weather-worn angular laterite stone guardian, clearly the heaviest, inspired by brick ruins and temple protector sculpture, stepped crown like a tiny prang, engraved orange laterite armor, thick low forearms, olive moss in the cracks and a single tarnished green bronze tablet set into chest, expressive stone eyes. Use four sharply different palette accents (ochre, rust, mint, brick-moss), distinctive weight and volumes, a kind of elegant playfulness even in large guardian. No loose items, no labels, no scenery and no floating particle effects.
